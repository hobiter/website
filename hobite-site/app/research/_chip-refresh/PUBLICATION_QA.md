- Results cut: TSM Q3 CY2026, NVDA Q3 FY2027, AVGO Q3 FY2026, AMD Q3 FY2026.

  five-year price return under the base remaining inputs; bear, base
  and bull values increase in that order at both horizons.

  archived databases. The five- and ten-year exit multiples are assumptions,
  not a replacement for the archived discounted cash flow model.
