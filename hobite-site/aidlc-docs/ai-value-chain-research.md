# AI Value Chain Research

Research cutoff: 2026-10-04; market observations: 2026-10-02 U.S. session.

## Requirements And Design

Create a substantial original English and Chinese study covering AI's physical supply chain, compute and networking, energy, cloud, foundation models, data/security, software, monetization, robotics and end-user productivity. Publish mirrored rich research pages in the two existing research libraries. Compare business/project ROIC with public-stock total return. Rank ten listed equities within a disclosed investable shortlist using transparent scenario returns, durability and downside assumptions; avoid claiming that a 20-year outcome is knowable.

Use primary issuer/SEC sources, dated prices and independent research. Keep reported, management-guided and analyst-modeled numbers visibly distinct. Forecast annual company revenue, operating profit, cash generation, capex, per-share economics and 10-/20-year returns with reproducible calculations. Include sensitivity, explicit share/ADS conventions, dilution, valuation compression, market-size reconciliation and conditions that invalidate conclusions.

Reuse Next.js and SVIM rich research registries. No new dependencies, credentials, database or infrastructure. Existing extension opt-outs and the standing authorization to complete research implementations and linked PRs apply. Inception, research/model design, implementation and build/browser verification execute; prior reverse-engineering artifacts remain applicable.
