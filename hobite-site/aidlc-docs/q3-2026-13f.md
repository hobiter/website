## Scope and requirements

Hobite only, English/Chinese research article. Curated 20 widely followed active institutional managers, not an objective fame or AUM ranking and not 20 mutual-fund products. Compare September 30 with June 30, using filings public through November 17. SEC's published Q3 deadline was November 16.

Produce cohort top-ten observed new positions, increases, reductions, exits and largest holdings, plus each manager's top-ten tables, share changes, percentage changes, portfolio weights and reported value totals. Manager counts and price-marked share-change proxies supplement, but do not substitute for, reported figures. Distinguish market-value changes from trading; do not invent cost bases, realized P&L or trade timestamps.

## Data design

- SEC submissions select correct-quarter 13F-HR and applicable amendments. Notices are not holdings reports. Archive primary and information XML; verify row counts and totals against summary values in dollars.
- Combine duplicate information-table lines by CUSIP / share type within a filing group. Exclude options and principal-value debt from share comparisons. Identify funds / non-common classes for transparent ranking exclusions.
- Compare comparable CUSIPs using shares, with null percentage for new positions and -100% for exits. Flag possible split/CUSIP/reorganization effects; do not silently call them trades.
- Implied unit price = reported value / shares. Change proxy = share delta x Q3 implied price for continuing/new positions, or Q2 price for exits. This is not cash spent or proceeds.
- Reconcile bases and restatements; supplemental holdings are additive only where their amendment semantics support it. Missing/uncertain reports remain visible, never represented as zero holdings.
- Cache source documents locally, preserve accession URLs, hashes, coverage and regeneration script. Publish derived summary JSON and downloadable comparison CSV for audit.
- No SVIM edits, no automatic recurring task and no live investment recommendation.
