## Objective

Apply `COMPANY_RESEARCH_STANDARD_WORKFLOW.md` to Tesla and publish matching English and Chinese research experiences in Hobite and SVIM. Use SEC filings as the numerical authority and Tesla investor-relations materials for operating metrics.

## Routes

- Hobite English: `/research/tesla-complete-fundamental-analysis`
- Hobite Chinese: `/research/tesla-complete-fundamental-analysis/zh`
- SVIM English: `/research/tesla`
- SVIM Chinese: `/research/tesla?lang=zh`

## Company-Specific Architecture

1. Separate automotive, energy generation and storage, and services revenue.
2. Track production, deliveries, vehicle inventory, FSD subscriptions, energy deployments and Supercharger infrastructure.
3. Separate automotive gross margin from regulatory-credit economics.
4. Track AI, factory and operational capital expenditure, free cash flow, cash and investments, debt and stock compensation.
5. Treat Robotaxi, Cybercab, FSD and Optimus as scenario-dependent options rather than established earnings streams.
6. Build bear, base and bull forecasts with DCF and market-multiple cross-checks.
7. Share typed research data across English and Chinese presentation layers.

## Source Hierarchy

1. Tesla Forms 10-K and 10-Q, SEC submissions and XBRL company facts.
2. Tesla quarterly updates and production, delivery and deployment releases.
3. Split-adjusted market prices for valuation context.
4. Hobite assumptions only for forecasts and scenario valuation.
